# HC Services — People Code
## Section 1: Annual Leave Policy

**Document Reference:** HC-PC-001  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025  
**Owner:** People & Culture Division

---

### 1.1 Policy Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of HC Services (the "Company") who have successfully completed their probationary period. Contractors, freelancers, and temporary staff are excluded unless their contract explicitly states otherwise.

---

### 1.2 Annual Leave Entitlement

**1.2.1 Standard Entitlement**  
All eligible employees are entitled to **21 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing at a rate of 1.75 days per month of service.

**1.2.2 Enhanced Entitlement by Tenure**  
Employees receive additional leave days based on years of continuous service:

| Years of Service | Additional Days | Total Entitlement |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 0 days | 21 days |
| 3–5 years | 3 days | 24 days |
| 6–9 years | 5 days | 26 days |
| 10+ years | 9 days | 30 days |

**1.2.3 Part-Time Employees**  
Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis calculated against the standard 21-day entitlement.

---

### 1.3 Leave Year & Accrual

**1.3.1 Leave Year Definition**  
The Company's leave year runs from **1 January to 31 December**. New joiners accrue leave from their start date on a pro-rata basis within the first calendar year.

**1.3.2 Accrual During Absence**  
Annual leave continues to accrue during:
- Approved sick leave (up to 90 days continuous)
- Maternity and paternity leave
- Approved study leave

Annual leave does **not** accrue during:
- Unpaid leave of absence exceeding 30 days
- Suspension pending investigation

---

### 1.4 Leave Request Procedure

**1.4.1 Advance Notice Requirement**  
Employees must submit annual leave requests **at least 5 working days** in advance for leave periods of less than 5 days. For leave periods of 5 or more consecutive working days, **at least 10 working days'** advance notice is required.

**1.4.2 Approval Process**  
Leave requests must be submitted via the HR Self-Service portal (Omni). The direct line manager has **3 working days** to approve or decline. If no response is received within 3 days, the leave is deemed **conditionally approved** pending HR review.

**1.4.3 Emergency Leave**  
In genuine emergency circumstances, employees may take leave with same-day notification to their manager. Documentation explaining the emergency must be submitted within **3 working days** of return.

---

### 1.5 Carry-Over & Forfeiture

**1.5.1 Carry-Over Allowance**  
Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 days** of unused annual leave to the following calendar year. Carried-over leave must be used by **31 March** of the following year or it will be forfeited.

**1.5.2 Forfeiture Exceptions**  
Leave may not be forfeited where:
- The Company directed the employee to postpone approved leave, in which case full carry-over applies.
- The employee was on approved sick leave during the planned period, in which case rescheduling is mandatory.

---

### 1.6 Leave Pay & Encashment

**1.6.1 Leave Pay**  
Annual leave is paid at the employee's **basic salary rate** plus fixed allowances. Variable pay, overtime, and bonuses are excluded.

**1.6.2 Leave Encashment**  
Leave encashment (converting unused leave to cash) is not permitted during active employment, except upon separation from the Company.

**1.6.3 Separation**  
Upon termination or resignation, any accrued but unused annual leave balance (up to the carry-over cap) will be paid out in the final settlement. Employees who resign before completing one year of service will have their entitlement pro-rated.

---

### 1.7 Public Holidays

Public holidays declared by the UAE Federal Government do not count as annual leave days. Employees required to work on a public holiday will receive a substitute day off within **30 days** of the public holiday.

---

### 1.8 Policy Violations

Unauthorised absence — defined as absence without approved leave or prior notification — will be treated as **unpaid leave** and may result in disciplinary action under the Company's Disciplinary Policy (HC-PC-006).
