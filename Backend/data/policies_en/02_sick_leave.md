### 2.1 Policy Scope

This policy applies to all employees of HC Services from their first day of service, **including those serving probation**, in accordance with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021. Entitlement during probation is subject to the payment restriction at §2.2.2.

---

### 2.2 Sick Leave Entitlement

**2.2.1 Entitlement and Pay Tranches**  
Employees are entitled to a maximum of **90 days** of sick leave per leave year. Pay depends on how many days of the 90 have already been taken in that year:

| Days of the 90 used | Pay level | Percentage | Counts toward the 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–15 | Full pay (basic + fixed allowances) | 100% | Yes |
| Days 16–60 | Half pay | 50% | Yes |
| Days 61–90 | Unpaid | 0% | Yes |

The 90-day entitlement is counted across a **rolling 12-month period**, defined at HC-PC-007 §7.5, not per calendar year.

**Worked example.** An employee who has taken 50 sick days in the rolling 12-month period is paid in full for days 1–15, at half pay for days 16–50 (35 days), and would move to unpaid from day 61 onward.

**2.2.2 Sick Leave During Probation**  
An employee serving probation accrues the full 90-day entitlement but is paid at **half pay from day one**, rather than the full-pay tranche at §2.2.1. Sick absence exceeding **30 calendar days** during probation extends the probationary period by the excess — see HC-PC-003 §3.6.1.

---

### 2.3 Notification & Documentation Requirements

**2.3.1 Notification**  
An employee unable to attend work must notify their line manager before **09:00** on the first day of absence, and on each subsequent day unless a medical certificate covering the period has been submitted.

**2.3.2 Medical Certification**  
Self-certification is permitted for absences of **1 or 2 consecutive days**. From the **third consecutive day**, an official medical certificate issued by a DHA, DOH, or MOH licensed practitioner is mandatory and must be uploaded to Omni within **48 hours** of the third day.

Absence of three or more consecutive days without a certificate is treated as unauthorised absence under HC-PC-001 §1.8.

**2.3.3 Certificates Issued Outside the UAE**  
A certificate issued outside the UAE must be attested by the relevant UAE consular authority, or accompanied by a report from a UAE-licensed practitioner, before the absence is recorded as certified sick leave.

---

### 2.4 Return to Work

An employee returning from three or more consecutive days of sick leave must complete a return-to-work discussion with their line manager on the first day back, recorded on **Form HR-S-01** (see HC-PC-007 §7.9). The discussion is supportive, not disciplinary.

---

### 2.5 Long-Term Illness

**2.5.1 Definition**  
Absence exceeding **20 consecutive working days** is treated as long-term illness and is managed jointly by the line manager and People & Culture.

**2.5.2 Capability Review**  
Where the 90-day entitlement in a rolling 12-month period is exhausted, the Company may commence a capability review under HC-PC-008 §8.2. A capability review is not a disciplinary process.

---

### 2.6 Absence Management

**2.6.1 Trigger Points**  
A review is triggered where an employee records **10 or more days** of sick absence, or **4 or more separate spells**, within a rolling 12-month period (HC-PC-007 §7.5).

**2.6.2 Bradford Factor**  
The Company uses the Bradford Factor to weight frequent short absences more heavily than a single long one:

**B = S² × D**, where **S** is the number of separate spells and **D** is the total days absent in the rolling 12-month period.

| Bradford score | Band | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–49 | No concern | None |
| 50–124 | Informal | Informal review with line manager |
| 125–399 | Formal | Formal counselling, recorded on file |
| 400 and above | Referral | Referral under HC-PC-006 §6.3 |

**Worked example.** Four separate spells totalling nine days gives B = 4² × 9 = **144**, which falls in the Formal band and requires recorded counselling — even though nine days is below the 10-day trigger at §2.6.1.

---

### 2.7 Abuse of Sick Leave

Falsifying a medical certificate, or claiming sick leave for a purpose other than genuine incapacity, is **gross misconduct** and is dealt with under HC-PC-006 §6.4.

### 2.9 Appendix A — Superseded Provisions (Version 2.8)

**SUPERSEDED — DO NOT APPLY TO ABSENCE ON OR AFTER 1 APRIL 2026.** The provision below was in force from 1 January 2025 to 31 March 2026 under Version 2.8. The provision currently in force is §2.2.1. This appendix is retained because absence occurring before 1 April 2026 is paid under it.

**A.1 Entitlement and Pay Tranches (Version 2.8, in force 1 January 2025 – 31 March 2026)**  
Employees are entitled to a maximum of 90 days of sick leave per year, paid as follows:

| Days of the 90 used | Pay level | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–15 | Full pay | 100% |
| Days 16–45 | Half pay | 50% |
| Days 46–90 | Unpaid | 0% |

Where a single period of absence straddles 1 April 2026, each day is paid under the rule in force on that day.
