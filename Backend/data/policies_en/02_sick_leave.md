# HC Services — People Code
## Section 2: Sick Leave Policy

**Document Reference:** HC-PC-002  
**Version:** 2.8  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025  
**Owner:** People & Culture Division

---

### 2.1 Policy Scope

This policy covers all employees of HC Services, including those on probation, regarding entitlements and procedures for sick leave (also referred to as medical leave). This policy complies with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 on the Regulation of Labour Relations and its executive regulations.

---

### 2.2 Sick Leave Entitlement

**2.2.1 Annual Sick Leave Allowance**  
All employees are entitled to a maximum of **90 days** of sick leave per year. Sick leave entitlement operates as follows:

| Period | Pay Level |
|---|---|
| First 15 days | Full pay (100% of basic salary + fixed allowances) |
| Days 16–45 | Half pay (50% of basic salary + fixed allowances) |
| Days 46–90 | Unpaid leave |

**2.2.2 Sick Leave During Probation**  
During the probationary period, sick leave is unpaid. The probationary period is not extended due to sick leave taken unless the total sick leave exceeds **30 calendar days**, in which case the excess days are added to the probation period.

---

### 2.3 Notification & Documentation Requirements

**2.3.1 Day-One Notification**  
Employees must notify their direct line manager and HR via the Omni portal **before 9:00 AM** on the first day of illness, or as soon as reasonably practicable.

**2.3.2 Medical Certificate**  
A medical certificate issued by a licensed medical practitioner must be submitted for:
- Any sick leave of **3 or more consecutive days**.
- Any sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday.
- Any pattern of frequent short-term absence (as determined by HR under Section 2.6).

**2.3.3 Approved Medical Facilities**  
For in-patient or extended illness, medical certificates from DHA-licensed (Dubai Health Authority) or DOH-licensed (Department of Health — Abu Dhabi) facilities are accepted. The Company reserves the right to request a second medical opinion at its own expense.

---

### 2.4 Return to Work

**2.4.1 Return-to-Work Interview**  
Upon return from sick leave of **5 or more consecutive working days**, employees must complete a Return-to-Work form and attend a brief return-to-work discussion with their line manager and an HR representative.

**2.4.2 Phased Return**  
Where medically recommended, HR may approve a phased return to work (e.g., reduced hours or modified duties) for up to **4 weeks**. This arrangement must be documented in writing and reviewed weekly.

---

### 2.5 Long-Term Illness

**2.5.1 Definition**  
Long-term illness is defined as a continuous period of certified illness exceeding **45 consecutive calendar days**.

**2.5.2 Management Process**  
- HR will initiate a formal case review after 30 continuous sick days.
- The Company will maintain regular contact with the employee and offer occupational health support.
- After 90 days of sick leave in a single year are exhausted, the Company may commence a review under the Capability Policy (HC-PC-008).

---

### 2.6 Absence Management

**2.6.1 Trigger Points**  
HR will initiate an informal absence review when an employee:
- Has **4 or more separate periods** of sick leave within a rolling 12-month period, regardless of duration; or
- Has taken **10 or more days** of sick leave within a rolling 12-month period.

**2.6.2 Bradford Factor**  
The Company uses the Bradford Factor formula (B = S² × D, where S = number of separate episodes, D = total days absent) as one of several indicators to identify patterns of short-term absence. A Bradford Factor score exceeding **100** triggers an HR review.

---

### 2.7 Abuse of Sick Leave

Providing false medical documentation, misrepresenting illness, or undertaking secondary employment during sick leave constitutes gross misconduct under the Disciplinary Policy (HC-PC-006) and may result in summary dismissal.
