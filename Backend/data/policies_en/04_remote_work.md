### 4.1 Policy Scope

This policy applies to employees who have **completed probation** (HC-PC-003 §3.3.2) and whose role is classified as remote-compatible under §4.2.2. Employees serving probation are not eligible, save under the exception at §4.5.2.

---

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria

**4.2.1 Performance Requirement**  
An employee is eligible where they have been confirmed in post and hold a current performance rating of **3 or above** on the five-point scale at HC-PC-007 §7.8. An employee on a formal performance or disciplinary process is not eligible for the duration of that process.

**4.2.2 Role Classification**  
Role classes are defined at HC-PC-007 §7.7. Remote allocation follows the class, not the grade:

| Role class | Examples | Maximum remote days per week |
|---|---|---|
| Class A — location independent | Consulting, analysis, software, design | 2 days |
| Class B — partially location dependent | Client delivery, People & Culture, Finance | 1 day |
| Class C — location dependent | Facilities management, reception, IT hardware support, security | 0 days |

A Class C role is not remote-compatible. Refusal on this ground is a matter of role classification, not of performance or conduct, and may be reviewed under §4.5.2.

---

### 4.3 Remote Work Arrangements

**4.3.1 Standard Pattern**  
Eligible employees work a **minimum of 3 days per week** in the office and a **maximum of 2 days per week** remotely, subject to the class limit at §4.2.2.

**4.3.2 Additional In-Office Requirements**  
A line manager may require additional in-office attendance for team meetings, client engagements, or project milestones, giving **24 hours'** notice.

**4.3.3 Working Abroad**  
Working remotely from outside the UAE requires **4 weeks'** advance approval from the line manager, People & Culture, and Legal, and is limited to **15 working days** per leave year. Approval is refused where the destination creates a tax or permanent-establishment risk. Employees relocating rather than travelling should refer to HC-PC-011, which is listed as unpublished at HC-PC-007 §7.10.

---

### 4.4 Employee Obligations During Remote Work

**4.4.1 Availability**  
Employees must be contactable and available during the core hours of **09:00 to 15:00 Gulf Standard Time**, and must record remote days in Omni in advance.

**4.4.2 Workspace and Security**  
Employees must work from a private, secure location. Company data may not be accessed over public or unsecured networks. Confidential material may not be printed at home. A security breach while working remotely is dealt with under HC-PC-006 §6.5.

---

### 4.5 Approval Process

**4.5.1 Standard Application**  
Applications are made on **Form HR-R-01** (HC-PC-007 §7.9) and approved by the line manager within **5 working days**. An approved arrangement is reviewed every **90 days**.

**4.5.2 Exceptions**  
People & Culture may grant an exception to the probation requirement at §4.1, or to the role classification at §4.2.2, on medical or caring grounds supported by documentation. An exception is time-limited and reviewed at each 90-day cycle.

---

### 4.6 Equipment & IT Support

**4.6.1 Equipment**  
The Company provides a laptop and, on request, a monitor and keyboard for home use. Equipment remains Company property and is returned on separation.

**4.6.2 Internet Allowance**  
An employee working **more than 6 remote days in a calendar month** may claim a monthly internet allowance of **AED 200**. The claim is submitted with a receipt through the expense process at HC-PC-005 §5.7.1 and is not subject to the prior-authorisation threshold at HC-PC-005 §5.2.1.

---

### 4.7 Policy Violations

Persistent failure to attend the office on the agreed pattern, or misrepresentation of remote working location, may result in withdrawal of the arrangement and disciplinary action under HC-PC-006 §6.5.

### 4.9 Appendix A — Superseded Provisions (Version 1.5)

**SUPERSEDED — DO NOT APPLY TO CLAIMS FOR MONTHS BEGINNING ON OR AFTER 1 JULY 2026.** The provision below was in force from 1 March 2025 to 30 June 2026 under Version 1.5. The provision currently in force is §4.6.2.

**A.1 Internet Allowance (Version 1.5, in force 1 March 2025 – 30 June 2026)**  
Employees working more than **8 remote days per month** may claim a monthly internet allowance of **AED 150**, subject to submission of receipts.
