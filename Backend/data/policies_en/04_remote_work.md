# HC Services — People Code
## Section 4: Remote Work & Flexible Working Policy

**Document Reference:** HC-PC-004  
**Version:** 1.5  
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025  
**Owner:** People & Culture Division

---

### 4.1 Policy Scope

This policy applies to all confirmed employees of HC Services who wish to work from a location other than their designated office. Remote work is a **privilege**, not a contractual right, and is subject to business needs and manager approval.

Employees on probation are not eligible for remote working arrangements unless an exception is granted by HR (Section 4.5.2).

---

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria

**4.2.1 Standard Eligibility**  
To be eligible for regular remote working, employees must:
- Have successfully completed their probationary period (Section 3.2).
- Have a performance rating of **"Meets Expectations"** or above in their most recent annual review.
- Have a role classified as **"Remote-Compatible"** by their department head, in consultation with IT.

**4.2.2 Ineligible Roles**  
Roles requiring physical presence — including but not limited to reception, facilities management, and lab-based roles — are not eligible for remote work arrangements.

---

### 4.3 Remote Work Arrangements

**4.3.1 Hybrid Schedule**  
The standard HC Services arrangement for eligible employees is **hybrid**, defined as:
- **Minimum 3 days per week** in the office.
- **Maximum 2 days per week** remote.

Line managers may require additional in-office days for team meetings, client engagements, or project milestones, with **24 hours'** notice.

**4.3.2 Full Remote (Exceptional Basis)**  
Full remote work (more than 2 remote days per week) requires:
- Written approval from the department head and CHRO.
- A documented business justification.
- A review every **90 days** to assess continuation.

**4.3.3 Remote Work Abroad**  
Working remotely from outside the UAE requires separate approval from HR and Legal due to tax, immigration, and data protection implications. Requests must be submitted at least **4 weeks in advance**.

---

### 4.4 Employee Obligations During Remote Work

**4.4.1 Availability**  
Remote employees must be available and responsive during their contracted working hours (typically 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM UAE time). Response to messages is expected within **60 minutes** during core hours.

**4.4.2 Data Security**  
Remote employees must:
- Use only HC Services-approved VPN when accessing company systems.
- Not work from public Wi-Fi networks without VPN enabled.
- Ensure physical documents are not visible to unauthorised persons.
- Use the encrypted laptop provided by IT and not personal devices for company work.

**4.4.3 Workspace Standards**  
Employees are responsible for maintaining a professional workspace. Video calls must be conducted from a professional background (virtual or physical). Video is expected to be on by default during team meetings.

---

### 4.5 Approval Process

**4.5.1 Standard Approval**  
Employees must submit a Remote Work Agreement (Form HR-R-01) via the Omni portal. The request must specify:
- Preferred remote days.
- Location of remote work.
- Equipment needs (if any).

Line manager approval is required within **5 working days**.

**4.5.2 Probation Exception**  
An employee on probation who has a medical, caregiving, or other documented need for remote work may submit a request to HR directly. Approval is at the joint discretion of the line manager and HR.

---

### 4.6 Equipment & IT Support

**4.6.1 Company Equipment**  
HC Services provides each employee with a laptop, headset, and access to collaboration tools (Teams, SharePoint, etc.). No additional hardware allowance is provided for remote work.

**4.6.2 Internet Allowance**  
Employees working more than **8 remote days per month** may claim a monthly internet allowance of **AED 150**, subject to submission of receipts via the Expense Claims portal (Section 5.4).

---

### 4.7 Policy Violations

Failure to comply with the data security, availability, or workspace standards outlined in this policy may result in:
- Revocation of remote work privileges.
- Formal disciplinary action under HC-PC-006.
