# HC Services — People Code
## Section 5: Expense Claims & Reimbursement Policy

**Document Reference:** HC-PC-005  
**Version:** 2.4  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025  
**Owner:** Finance & People & Culture Division

---

### 5.1 Policy Scope

This policy governs the reimbursement of legitimate business expenses incurred by HC Services employees in the course of their employment. All claims must represent genuine, necessary, and reasonable business expenditures.

---

### 5.2 General Principles

**5.2.1 Authorisation Before Expenditure**  
Where possible, employees should obtain prior authorisation from their line manager before incurring any expense exceeding **AED 500** per transaction.

**5.2.2 Receipts**  
Original receipts (digital or physical) are required for **all** claims. Credit card statements are not accepted as standalone proof of expenditure.

**5.2.3 Submission Deadline**  
Expense claims must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days may be declined at the Finance team's discretion.

---

### 5.3 Travel Expenses

**5.3.1 Business Travel Approval**  
All inter-city and international travel must be pre-approved using Form FIN-T-01 via Omni before booking is made.

**5.3.2 Flight Bookings**  
Domestic flights must be booked in **Economy class**. International flights for journeys under **5 hours** must be Economy class. International flights for journeys **5 hours or more** may be booked in Business class for employees at Grade 6 (Manager) and above. All others must book Economy.

**5.3.3 Hotel Accommodation**  
Hotel accommodation caps apply by city:

| City Tier | Nightly Cap (AED, excl. taxes) |
|---|---|
| Dubai / Abu Dhabi | AED 750 |
| Other UAE | AED 500 |
| MENA Region | AED 600 |
| Europe / USA | AED 900 |
| Other International | AED 700 |

**5.3.4 Ground Transport**  
Taxis and ride-hailing (Careem, Uber) are reimbursable. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at **AED 0.67 per km** (ADNOC rate), subject to submission of distance evidence.

---

### 5.4 Daily Allowances (Per Diem)

When on pre-approved overnight business travel, employees receive a per diem in lieu of meal receipts:

| Location | Per Diem (AED/day) |
|---|---|
| Within UAE | AED 150 |
| MENA Region | AED 250 |
| Europe / USA | AED 350 |
| Other International | AED 280 |

Per diem is not payable on the day of return if the employee reaches their home city before 6:00 PM local time.

---

### 5.5 Entertainment & Client Hospitality

**5.5.1 Limits**  
Client entertainment expenses must be pre-approved and are capped at:
- **AED 250 per head** for meals.
- **AED 500 per head** for events (sports, cultural events, etc.).

**5.5.2 Documentation Requirements**  
Claims for client entertainment must include:
- Names and organisations of all attendees.
- Business purpose of the entertainment.
- Original receipt.

---

### 5.6 Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are explicitly excluded from reimbursement:
- Personal grooming, spa, gym, or leisure activities.
- Alcoholic beverages (unless explicitly part of a client entertainment event approved in advance).
- Fines, penalties, or legal costs incurred through personal negligence.
- Expenses incurred by a spouse or family member.
- Upgrades beyond approved travel class.

---

### 5.7 Submission & Payment Process

**5.7.1 Submission**  
Claims are submitted via the Omni Expense portal. Supporting receipts must be attached as clear photographs or scans (PDF, JPG, PNG).

**5.7.2 Approval Workflow**  
- Claims below **AED 1,000**: Line manager approval only.
- Claims **AED 1,000–5,000**: Line manager + Finance review.
- Claims above **AED 5,000**: Line manager + Finance + CFO approval.

**5.7.3 Payment**  
Approved expense claims are reimbursed via the employee's payroll in the **following month's payroll cycle**, or as an ad-hoc payment for urgent claims above AED 2,000, subject to Finance availability.

---

### 5.8 Fraud & Misuse

Submitting false, duplicate, or inflated expense claims constitutes gross misconduct under the Disciplinary Policy (HC-PC-006) and may result in summary dismissal, legal action, and recovery of funds.
