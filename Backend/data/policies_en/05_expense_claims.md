### 5.1 Policy Scope

This policy applies to all employees incurring expenditure on Company business. It is owned jointly by Finance and People & Culture; where the two differ on a point of interpretation, Finance prevails on matters of amount and People & Culture on matters of eligibility (HC-PC-007 §7.1).

---

### 5.2 General Principles

**5.2.1 Prior Authorisation**  
Prior written authorisation from the line manager is required before incurring any single expense exceeding **AED 500**. The internet allowance at HC-PC-004 §4.6.2 is expressly exempt.

**5.2.2 Submission Deadline**  
Claims must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted later may be declined at the discretion of Finance.

**5.2.3 Evidence**  
Every claim requires an itemised receipt. A card statement is not a receipt.

---

### 5.3 Travel Expenses

**5.3.1 Booking**  
Travel is booked through the Company travel desk using **Form FIN-T-01** (HC-PC-007 §7.9). Self-booked travel is reimbursed only up to the fare the travel desk would have obtained.

**5.3.2 Cabin Class**  
Domestic flights are Economy for all grades. For international flights:

| Flight duration | Grades 1–5 | Grade 6 and above |
|---|---|---|
| Under 5 hours | Economy | Economy |
| 5 hours or more | Economy | Business |

Grade bands are defined at HC-PC-007 §7.6. Eligibility follows **grade, not seniority of title**.

**5.3.3 Accommodation**  
Nightly caps exclude taxes and municipality fees:

| City tier | Nightly cap (AED) | Maximum nights without VP approval |
|---|---|---|
| Dubai / Abu Dhabi | 750 | 5 |
| Other UAE | 500 | 5 |
| MENA region | 600 | 7 |
| Europe / USA | 900 | 7 |
| Other international | 700 | 10 |

**5.3.4 Ground Transport**  
Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursable. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at **AED 0.67 per kilometre**, with distance evidence.

---

### 5.4 Daily Allowances (Per Diem)

Per diem covers meals and incidentals and is paid for each **full day** away, and for the day of departure. It is **not** payable for the day of return:

| Location | Per diem (AED/day) | Payable on return day |
|---|---|---|
| Within UAE | 150 | No |
| MENA region | 250 | No |
| Europe / USA | 350 | No |
| Other international | 280 | No |

**Worked example.** An employee departing Monday and returning Thursday from London claims per diem for Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday — three days at AED 350 = **AED 1,050**. Thursday is the return day and is not payable.

---

### 5.5 Entertainment & Client Hospitality

**5.5.1 Client Meals**  
Client entertainment is reimbursable up to **AED 250 per head**, including the employee. The claim must name every attendee.

**5.5.2 Alcohol**  
Alcohol is reimbursable only where a client is present and only up to 25% of the total bill.

---

### 5.6 Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursable: traffic and parking fines; personal grooming; gym and health club membership; in-flight purchases other than meals; hotel minibar; and personal travel insurance. A claim for a non-reimbursable item is rejected with a reference to this section and may be appealed under HC-PC-009 §9.2.

---

### 5.7 Submission & Payment Process

**5.7.1 Submission**  
Claims are submitted through the Omni Expense portal with receipts attached. This is the route for the internet allowance under HC-PC-004 §4.6.2.

**5.7.2 Approval Thresholds**  
The approval required depends on the total value of the claim, assessed under the version of this policy in force on the **date the expense was incurred** (§5.0):

| Claim total (AED) | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Under 1,500 | Line manager |
| 1,500 – 7,500 | Line manager and Finance |
| Above 7,500 | Line manager, Finance, and the Chief Financial Officer |

An employee may not approve their own claim, nor a claim from which they benefit.

**Worked example.** A claim for AED 4,000 incurred on 15 February 2026 falls in the middle band and needs the line manager and Finance. The same AED 4,000 incurred on 15 November 2025 would fall under the superseded thresholds at §5.9 — above AED 1,000 but below AED 5,000 — and would have needed the same two approvers. A claim for AED 1,200 reverses the result: line manager only today, line manager and Finance in 2025.

**5.7.3 Payment**  
Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run, provided approval completes 5 working days before the payroll cut-off.

---

### 5.8 Fraud & Misuse

Submitting a claim for an expense not incurred, inflating an amount, or submitting a receipt for a personal expense is **gross misconduct** under HC-PC-006 §6.4.
