### 7.1 How to Read the People Code

Where two provisions appear to conflict, they are resolved in this order:

1. **UAE federal law prevails over any provision of this Code.** Where a provision grants less than the statutory minimum, the statutory minimum applies.
2. **An individual contract prevails where it is more generous** than this Code. It may never be less generous.
3. **A specific provision prevails over a general one.** A rule about probation in HC-PC-003 prevails over a general rule in HC-PC-001.
4. **The version in force at the time of the event prevails.** A rule is applied as it stood on the date of the event it governs — the date leave accrued, the date an expense was incurred, the date of absence — not as it stands today. Superseded provisions are retained in each policy's §N.9 appendix for this purpose.
5. Where a conflict remains, People & Culture decides on eligibility and Finance decides on amount.

### 7.2 Continuous Service

**Continuous service** runs from the employee's official start date and is not broken by approved annual leave, approved sick leave, maternity or paternity leave, approved study leave, or an internal transfer (HC-PC-003 §3.2.3).

It **is** broken by resignation and re-hire, and by unpaid leave of absence exceeding 30 consecutive days, which does not count toward service.

Continuous service determines annual leave entitlement under HC-PC-001 §1.2.2 and accrual during absence under HC-PC-001 §1.3.2.

### 7.3 Day Definitions

These three terms are not interchangeable, and the difference changes the answer:

| Term | Meaning | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| **Working day** | Monday to Friday, excluding UAE public holidays declared at HC-PC-001 §1.7 | Leave notice (HC-PC-001 §1.4.1), probation extension notice (HC-PC-003 §3.6.2), expense approval SLA |
| **Calendar day** | Every day including weekends and public holidays | Probation notice (HC-PC-003 §3.4), sick absence during probation (HC-PC-002 §2.2.2), expense submission deadline (HC-PC-005 §5.2.2) |
| **Consecutive day** | Unbroken calendar days including weekends | Medical certification (HC-PC-002 §2.3.2) |

All times are **Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4)** unless stated otherwise.

### 7.4 Pay Definitions

**Basic salary** is the fixed monthly amount stated in the contract of employment, excluding all allowances.

**Fixed allowances** are allowances paid at the same amount every month regardless of activity: housing allowance, transport allowance, and any role-based allowance stated in the contract.

The following are **not** fixed allowances: overtime, performance bonus, commission, project completion payments, the annual air ticket allowance (HC-PC-003 §3.5.4), and the internet allowance (HC-PC-004 §4.6.2).

Leave pay under HC-PC-001 §1.6.1 is basic salary plus fixed allowances only.

---

### 7.5 Rolling 12-Month Period

A **rolling 12-month period** is the 12 months immediately preceding the date of assessment — not the calendar year. An assessment made on 15 August 2026 looks back to 16 August 2025.

**Worked example.** An employee took 6 sick days in November 2025 and 5 in June 2026. Assessed on 15 August 2026, the rolling period captures both, totalling 11 days — which exceeds the 10-day trigger at HC-PC-002 §2.6.1. Assessed on 15 December 2026, the November 2025 absence has dropped out and only 5 days count.

### 7.6 Grade Bands

The grade recorded against an employee determines travel entitlement, probation length, and expense authority. Job titles are indicative; **the grade governs**.

| Grade | Band | Typical titles | Business class on flights of 5h+ | Standard probation | Own expense authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Support | Coordinator, Administrator, Supervisor | No | 6 months | None |
| 3 | Professional | Analyst, Associate Analyst | No | 6 months | None |
| 4 | Professional | Consultant, Senior Analyst | No | 6 months | Up to AED 1,500 |
| 5 | Senior Professional | Senior Consultant, Team Lead | No | 6 months | Up to AED 1,500 |
| 6 | Management | Manager, Principal Consultant | **Yes** | 6 months | Up to AED 7,500 |
| 7 | Leadership | Director, Senior Manager | Yes | **Up to 12 months** | Up to AED 7,500 |
| 8 | Leadership | Senior Director, Vice President | Yes | Up to 12 months | Above AED 7,500 |
| 9 | Executive | Executive Director, Chief Officer | Yes | Up to 12 months | Above AED 7,500 |

The business class threshold is set at Grade 6 by HC-PC-005 §5.3.2. The extended probation threshold is set at Grade 7 by HC-PC-003 §3.2.2. **These are different thresholds** — a Grade 6 Manager flies business but serves the standard 6-month probation.

### 7.7 Role Classification

Roles are classified for remote working by how far the work depends on physical location, not by grade or seniority.

**Class A — location independent.** The work can be performed anywhere with a secure connection: consulting, analysis, software engineering, design, most People & Culture and Finance work.

**Class B — partially location dependent.** The work requires regular physical presence with clients or colleagues: client delivery management, recruitment, office-based Finance operations.

**Class C — location dependent.** The work cannot be performed away from Company or client premises: facilities management, reception, IT hardware support, security, and site supervision.

Classification is recorded against the role, not the person, and is applied at HC-PC-004 §4.2.2.

### 7.8 Performance Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | Consistently exceeds every requirement of the role |
| 4 | Exceeds | Exceeds the requirements of the role in most areas |
| 3 | Meets | Meets the requirements of the role |
| 2 | Partially meets | Falls short in one or more material areas; improvement plan required |
| 1 | Does not meet | Falls materially short; managed under HC-PC-008 |

A rating of **3 or above** is the threshold for probation confirmation (HC-PC-003 §3.3.2) and for remote work eligibility (HC-PC-004 §4.2.1).
