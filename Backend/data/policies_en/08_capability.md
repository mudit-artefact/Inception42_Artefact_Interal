### 8.1 Policy Scope

This policy applies to confirmed employees whose performance or health prevents them meeting the requirements of their role. It is **not** a disciplinary process — conduct is dealt with under HC-PC-006. Performance during probation is dealt with under HC-PC-003.

---

### 8.2 Medical Capability Review

Where an employee has exhausted the 90-day sick leave entitlement within a rolling 12-month period (HC-PC-002 §2.5.2, HC-PC-007 §7.5), the Company may commence a medical capability review.

The review considers occupational health advice, whether reasonable adjustments would enable a return, and whether redeployment is available. Dismissal on medical capability grounds is a last resort and carries full contractual notice.

An employee under medical capability review continues to accrue annual leave (HC-PC-001 §1.3.2) and is not eligible for remote working while the review is open (HC-PC-004 §4.2.1).

---

### 8.3 Performance Improvement

An employee rated **2 or below** on the scale at HC-PC-007 §7.8 is placed on a performance improvement plan of up to 3 months, with documented objectives and support. An employee on a performance improvement plan is not eligible for remote working (HC-PC-004 §4.2.1).

---

### 8.4 Right of Appeal

A capability outcome may be appealed within **10 working days** under HC-PC-009 §9.2.
