### 9.1 Policy Scope

This policy covers any concern, problem, or complaint an employee wishes to raise about their employment, and every appeal against a decision taken under this Code.

---

### 9.2 Raising a Grievance or Appeal

**9.2.1 Informal Stage**  
An employee is encouraged to raise the matter with their line manager first. Where the grievance concerns the line manager, it may be raised directly with People & Culture.

**9.2.2 Formal Stage**  
A formal grievance is submitted in writing to People & Culture. A hearing is held within **10 working days** of receipt, and a written outcome issued within a further **5 working days**.

**9.2.3 Appeals**  
Any decision under HC-PC-003 (probation), HC-PC-005 (a rejected expense claim), HC-PC-006 (a disciplinary sanction), or HC-PC-008 (a capability outcome) may be appealed within **10 working days** of the written outcome. An appeal is heard by a manager who was not involved in the original decision.

Raising a grievance does not pause a probationary period (HC-PC-003 §3.7), and does not suspend a disciplinary sanction unless People & Culture directs otherwise.

---

### 9.3 Protection from Detriment

No employee will be subjected to detriment for raising a grievance in good faith. Retaliation against an employee who has raised a grievance is gross misconduct under HC-PC-006 §6.4.
