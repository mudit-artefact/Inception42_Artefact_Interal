### 12.1 Policy Scope

This policy covers the education allowance paid towards the schooling of an employee's dependent children, and the proof of schooling an employee must submit to claim it. It applies to every employee holding an education allowance entitlement under the Standard or Enhanced plan.

Claims are assessed against the plan and the academic cycle in force on the submission deadline, in line with the precedence rules at HC-PC-007 §7.1.

---

### 12.2 Eligibility

**12.2.1 Employment Status**  
An employee is eligible while their employment status is **Active** or **On Leave**. Approved leave does not interrupt entitlement.

**12.2.2 Employment Dates**  
The employee must have joined on or before the submission deadline, and must not have left before the submission window opened. The dates decide eligibility, not the wording of the leaver record.

**12.2.3 Eligible Dependants**  
The allowance is payable in respect of an employee's **children** only. The child must be recorded as an **Active** dependant and **Enrolled** at school in the employee's HR record. A dependant recorded as inactive, or not enrolled, is not eligible.

**12.2.4 Employees Without an Entitlement**  
An employee whose benefits package carries no education plan has no entitlement under this policy. Where an employee believes their package is recorded incorrectly, they should contact People & Culture.

---

### 12.3 The Annual Limit

**12.3.1 Limit by Plan**  
The allowance is capped per eligible child, per academic year:

| Plan | Annual limit per eligible child |
|---|---|
| Education Allowance – Standard | AED 25,000 |
| Education Allowance – Enhanced | AED 45,000 |
| No education plan | Not applicable |

**12.3.2 How the Limit Applies**  
The limit applies to each eligible child separately. It is a ceiling on what may be reimbursed, not an entitlement to that amount: only charges actually incurred and evidenced are reimbursed, and only those falling within §12.4.

**12.3.3 Which Plan Applies**  
An employee's plan is recorded in their HR record. An employee may ask the HCS Concierge which plan applies to them.

---

### 12.4 What May Be Claimed

**12.4.1 Claimable Charges**  
The following charges are claimable under both plans:

| Charge | Claimable |
|---|---|
| Tuition and academic instruction | Both plans |
| Registration and enrolment fees | Both plans |
| Examinations and assessments | Both plans |
| School activities and enrichment | Both plans |
| Books and learning materials | **Enhanced plan only** |

**12.4.2 Charges That Are Not Claimable**  
Any charge that does not fall within a category listed at §12.4.1 is not claimable. Uniform, transport, catering, private tuition outside the school, and voluntary contributions are common examples. Where an invoice mixes claimable and non-claimable charges, only the claimable lines count towards the limit.

**12.4.3 One Claim per Child per Year**  
An academic year attracts one approved claim per eligible child. Where the same charge has already been claimed — by this employee or elsewhere — it is not payable again.

---

### 12.5 Documents Required

**12.5.1 The Four Documents**  
A complete claim consists of four documents:

| Document | What it must state |
|---|---|
| Enrolment certificate | The child's name, date of birth, the school's name, the academic year, and the date of issue |
| Itemised school invoice | The child's name, the school's name, and the date of issue |
| Payment receipt | The date the payment was made |
| Employee declaration | That the employee has not claimed the same charges from another employer |

**12.5.2 Form and Legibility**  
Documents are submitted as PDF, PNG or JPEG, each no larger than 10 MB. A document that cannot be read — a poor scan, a photograph taken in low light, a page with a field obscured — will be returned for a clearer copy.

**12.5.3 Authority**  
The enrolment certificate must carry either the school's stamp or the name and role of the person signing it.

**12.5.4 Partial Submissions**  
A claim may be submitted in parts. It is not assessed until all four documents have arrived; until then the employee is told which are still needed.

---

### 12.6 The Academic Cycle

**12.6.1 The 2026–2027 Cycle**  
Claims for the 2026–2027 academic year are governed by the following dates:

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Submission window opens | 1 August 2026 |
| Submission deadline | 15 October 2026 |
| Review completed by | 15 November 2026 |
| Payroll cut-off | 30 November 2026 |

**12.6.2 Document Issue Dates**  
A document is accepted only if it was issued between **1 June 2026** and **31 May 2027**. A document from an earlier academic year is not accepted, even where the charges are otherwise claimable.

**12.6.3 Late Submissions**  
A claim submitted after the deadline at §12.6.1 is not payable for that academic year.

**12.6.4 Reminders**  
Employees with an open claim are reminded at 56, 28, 14 and 7 days before the submission deadline.

---

### 12.7 How a Claim Is Assessed

**12.7.1 What Is Checked**  
Every claim is checked for the following, and the employee is told which check a problem relates to:

| Check | What it confirms |
|---|---|
| Completeness | All four documents at §12.5.1 have arrived |
| Required detail | Each document states what §12.5.1 requires of it |
| Identity | The names and date of birth on the documents match the HR record |
| Consistency | The documents agree with one another — same child, same school, same academic year, and the receipt settles the invoice it cites |
| Timing | The issue dates fall within §12.6.2 and the claim arrived by the deadline |
| Duplication | The same child has no approved claim for this year, and the invoice has not already been settled elsewhere |

**12.7.2 Outcomes**  
A claim where every check passes is approved without a human step. Any other claim is referred to People & Culture, who decide. A referral is not a refusal.

**12.7.3 Correcting a Claim**  
Where a document is returned, the employee removes it and submits a corrected copy. The claim is reassessed in full. There is no limit on the number of corrections before the deadline at §12.6.1.

---

### 12.8 Decisions and Appeals

**12.8.1 Notification**  
An employee is told the outcome of their claim, and where it was not approved, which check it did not meet and what would resolve it.

**12.8.2 Payment**  
An approved claim is paid through payroll, in the cycle following approval and no later than the payroll cut-off at §12.6.1.

**12.8.3 Appeals**  
A decision under this policy may be appealed within **10 working days** of the written outcome, under HC-PC-009 §9.2.3. An appeal is heard by a manager who was not involved in the original decision.
