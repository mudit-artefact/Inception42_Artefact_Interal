### 13.1 Policy Scope

This policy covers the documents a new joiner must provide so that HC Services can apply for their UAE employment visa, and how those documents are checked.

It applies **before** the employee's first day. It does not cover the renewal of an existing residence visa, the sponsorship of family members, or any matter arising after employment has begun; those are handled by People & Culture directly and are not provided for in this Code.

---

### 13.2 Who This Applies To

**13.2.1 Employment Status**  
This policy applies to a person whose employment status is **Onboarding** — an accepted offer, a start date agreed, and employment not yet commenced.

**13.2.2 What Onboarding Means for Everything Else**  
A person who is Onboarding has not begun continuous service under HC-PC-007 §7.2. They therefore have no leave entitlement, no probation period in progress, and no expense authority. Leave begins to accrue on the first day of employment under HC-PC-001 §1.3.1, and probation begins on the same date under HC-PC-003 §3.2.1.

An Onboarding person may ask about any policy in this Code. They may not apply for leave, cancel leave, or approve anyone else's.

**13.2.3 When This Policy Stops Applying**  
On the first day of employment the status changes to Active and this policy ceases to apply to that person.

---

### 13.3 The Two Visa Routes

Which route applies is decided by the role, and is recorded against the person before the case is opened.

| Route | Applies to | Academic certificate required |
|---|---|---|
| Employment visa — degree required | Roles whose licensing or classification requires a recognised qualification | Yes |
| Employment visa — no degree required | All other roles | No |

**13.3.1 Which Route Applies**  
The route is recorded on the person's case. A new joiner may ask the HCS Concierge which route applies to them rather than assuming from the table above.

---

### 13.4 The Documents

**13.4.1 What Is Required**

| Document | Required on | What it must show |
|---|---|---|
| Passport copy | Both routes | The full name, date of birth, nationality, passport number, place of issue, and the issue and expiry dates |
| Photograph | Both routes | The face, in colour, on a white background |
| Signed job-offer form | Both routes | The employer, the job title, the start date, and a signature with the date it was signed |
| Attested academic certificate | Degree route only | The awarding institution, the qualification, the date awarded, and the attestation stamps |

**13.4.2 How Many**  
Four documents on the degree route, three on the other. Nothing else is required, and a document not named above is not read.

The condition each one must satisfy is at §13.7.1.

---

### 13.5 Where to Send Them

Documents are submitted through the HCS Concierge, which opens the visa document window and shows what the case still needs. A visa document is not an expense claim and is not a school document; each has its own window and its own checks.

**13.5.1 Partial Submissions**  
Documents may be sent in parts. The case is not assessed until every document required by the route has arrived; until then the new joiner is told which are still outstanding.

---

### 13.6 The Deadline

**13.6.1 The 2026 Cohort**  
Documents for the 2026 joining cohort must be submitted by **7 October 2026**.

**13.6.2 Late Submissions**  
A case not complete by the deadline at §13.6.1 cannot be lodged in that filing round, and the start date may be moved. Where a document cannot be obtained in time, the new joiner should tell People & Culture before the deadline rather than after it.

---

### 13.7 How a Case Is Assessed

**13.7.1 What Is Checked**  
Once every required document has arrived, the case is checked for all of the following. A document that fails a check is returned; failing one does not change the route.

| Check | What it requires |
|---|---|
| Passport validity | The passport is valid for at least **6 months** from the date the case is assessed. A passport with less than that must be renewed first |
| Photograph usable | In colour, on a white background, showing one face looking at the camera. A photograph showing more than one person is returned |
| Offer signed | The job-offer form issued by HC Services, signed and dated. An unsigned form is returned |
| Certificate attested | The academic certificate carries attestation — degree route only. A genuine certificate that is not attested is returned for attestation |
| Same person | Every document names the new joiner. A difference in spelling between documents is a reason to return them, because the application is made in one name |
| Offer is this employer | The offer is from the hiring entity. A form naming a different employer is returned |

**13.7.2 What Each Status Means**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Awaiting Submission | One or more required documents has not arrived |
| Ready for the PRO | Every document has arrived and every check has passed; the case has been passed to the public relations officer to lodge |

**13.7.3 Who Decides**  
A case where every check passes is progressed without a reviewer. A case where any check fails is held and looked at by People & Culture.

---

### 13.8 If Something Is Wrong

**13.8.1 A Returned Document**  
Where a document is returned, the new joiner removes it and submits a corrected copy. The case is reassessed in full. There is no limit on the number of corrections before the deadline at §13.6.1.

**13.8.2 Being Told Why**  
A new joiner is told which document was returned and what is wrong with it. They are not asked to guess, and they are not told a case has failed without being told which document caused it.

**13.8.3 Questions and Complaints**  
A new joiner who disagrees with a decision under this policy may raise it with People & Culture. The grievance procedure at HC-PC-009 applies to employees; a person who has not yet started should write to People & Culture directly.
